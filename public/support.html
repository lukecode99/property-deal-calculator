Common questions

Where do the figures in Due Diligence come from?
Sold prices come from HM Land Registry Price Paid data, flood risk from the Environment Agency, planning records from local authority registers, energy ratings from the EPC register, crime from police.uk, stations from Department for Transport NaPTAN data, local housing allowance rates from the VOA, employment figures from the ONS, and schools from Department for Education data. All of it is public open data.

Why is some of the data out of date?
Land Registry sold prices are published in arrears and can run a few months behind completion, so a recent sale may not appear yet. Where a source is behind, the app says so on the relevant tab. If a lookup fails, the app tells you it failed rather than showing a blank result — a blank means genuinely nothing found.

Are the tax calculations advice?
No. Stamp duty, Section 24 relief, corporation tax and ICR stress figures are calculated to current published rules and are there to help you compare deals. They are not tax or financial advice, and you should confirm anything you are relying on with a qualified adviser before you commit to a purchase.

Where are my saved deals stored?
On your device only. They are not backed up to us and they are not synced between devices. Deleting the app deletes them. Use Share or the CSV export to keep a copy.

How do I remove the adverts?
There is currently no paid ad-free option. Adverts are requested in non-personalised mode. See the privacy policy for what that means.
