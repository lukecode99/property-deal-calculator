Property Deal Calculator

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 30 July 2026

The short version

There is no account and no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other identifying detail. The app does send the postcode you type to third-party data services in order to look up information about that area, and it shows adverts. Both are described in full below.

Postcode lookups

The Due Diligence section works by sending the postcode you enter to a set of lookup services. A postcode identifies an area rather than a person, and we do not store it or associate it with you or your device. The services used are:

  • postcodes.io — converts a postcode to a map location.
  • Our own lookup services (hosted on Cloudflare Workers), which draw on HM Land Registry Price Paid data, Environment Agency flood risk, local authority planning records, the EPC register, police.uk street-level crime, Department for Transport NaPTAN station data, VOA Local Housing Allowance rates, ONS employment data, and Department for Education school data.

These requests carry only the postcode. Our services keep short-lived caches of the responses so that repeat searches are faster; those caches are keyed on the postcode alone and contain no information about who asked. Standard web server logs may record the request and the originating IP address for a short period as part of ordinary operation and abuse prevention.

Advertising

The app displays a banner advert supplied by Google AdMob. Adverts are requested in non-personalised mode, which means Google is instructed not to use any profile of your interests to select them. Google still processes limited data to serve and measure adverts, including your device's IP address and general information about the device, and it may use a non-resettable or session-scoped identifier for frequency capping and fraud prevention. We do not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and we do not do so.

Google's handling of that data is covered by its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter about a deal — purchase price, rent, refurbishment costs, saved deals and comparisons — is calculated and stored entirely on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

When you share or export a deal as a PDF or CSV, the file is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to send it. We are not involved in that.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on request. If you want the adverts to stop entirely, deleting the app removes them. Google's own controls are at My Ad Center.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top of this page will change and the updated policy will be published here before the change takes effect in a released version.
