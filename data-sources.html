Property Deal Calculator — Data Sources & Licensing

Bundle ID: com.lukeholder.propertydealcalc · United Kingdom · Last reviewed 6 November 2026

This document lists every external data source used by Property Deal Calculator, what each one provides, and the licence under which we are permitted to use it. The app is a calculation and information tool for the United Kingdom only. It provides illustrative figures based on numbers the user enters plus publicly available data; it does not give regulated financial, mortgage, tax or investment advice and does not transact or arrange any financial product.

The majority of our data is UK public-sector open data published under the Open Government Licence v3.0 (OGL). The OGL explicitly grants the right to copy, publish, distribute, adapt and exploit the information commercially, provided the source is acknowledged. Full licence text: nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3. No scraped, proprietary or restricted third-party datasets are used.

Access notes & authorisation

All sources above are open to the public and licensed for reuse, including commercial reuse, under the terms linked. The only source that requires an account to obtain data in bulk is the EPC register, which we access through the official GOV.UK "Get energy performance data" service using a registered GOV.UK One Login account; the data itself remains licensed under OGL v3.0. No source requires a commercial agreement, paid licence, or special authorisation that we do not hold.
